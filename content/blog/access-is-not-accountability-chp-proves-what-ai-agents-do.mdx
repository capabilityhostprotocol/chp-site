# Access is Not Accountability

In the evolving world of AI agents, there's a critical distinction that often gets overlooked: **access is not accountability**.

When an AI agent is granted access to perform tasks, it opens the door to action. However, this access alone does not equate to accountability. Accountability requires something more — it demands **proof of what was done**, not just permission to act.

At Capability Host Protocol (CHP), we understand this difference and have built a system that governs AI agents while also providing **signed, denial-aware evidence** of their actions. This means we don't just allow AI agents to operate — we ensure that every action they take is verifiable and traceable.

No hype. No invented facts or metrics. Just a clear, transparent process that ensures accountability through proof.

By separating access from accountability, CHP empowers organizations to harness the potential of AI agents without compromising on governance or transparency.
